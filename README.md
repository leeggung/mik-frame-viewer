# MIK FRAME VIEWER v2.0.0

## What is this?

MIK FRAME VIEWER is a desktop app for enjoying your own photos, music, comics, and novels inside beautifully decorated "rooms." It's not just a media player — you decorate your own room, collect items, and invite friends to visit and see how you've styled it.

You can share it with someone you miss or long to see. Creators can also use timed friend invites to serialize their own work — music, illustrations, comics, stories, and more. The app is completely free.

## Key Features

- 🖼️ **Frame Room** — Hang your photos in a frame and enjoy them as a slideshow
- 📚 **Library** — Shelve your comics and read them from the bookcase
- 🎵 **Music Room** — Play your favorite tracks with album art, visualizers, and party-mode effects
- 📕 **Novel Room** — Shelve and read your novels/text files, with text-to-speech narration
- 🖼️ **Picture Book Room** — New in 2.0! Turn your novels into illustrated picture books with page art and character voices
- 👥 **Friend Rooms** — Visit friends' rooms and see their decorations, music, and collections come to life

## What's New in 2.0

- Added the Picture Book decorating feature to the Novel Room
- Fixed background music not stopping when picture books or novel narration plays
- Made save files crash-safe (no more data loss on unexpected close)
- Fixed music room effect items not showing up correctly in friend rooms
- Added new decorative items
